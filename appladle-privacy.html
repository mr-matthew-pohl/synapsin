AppLadle Privacy Policy

Plainly: AppLadle keeps your data on your device — we never receive it.

Last updated 17 July 2026.

The short version

AppLadle is a private, offline workspace for small apps, tools, and games. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no accounts. Everything you create stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

The data you create — profiles, notebook entries, calculator history, game scores, the mini-apps you build or install (and their local storage), reminders, and home-screen widget content — is stored in an encrypted database (AES-256) on your device. The encryption key is protected by your device's hardware-backed Android Keystore. This data never leaves your device except through actions you explicitly take (see below), and it is excluded from Android cloud backup.

Data sent off your device

The app makes no network requests on its own. The only time information leaves your device is when you use the optional “Create with Gemini” feature:

  • You supply your own Google AI Studio API key.
  • When you tap Create with Gemini, the prompt and any current code you're editing are sent over HTTPS to Google's Generative Language API using your key.
  • That request is governed by Google's own privacy policy. AppLadle does not receive, store, or forward this data anywhere else.

Mini-apps you build or install run in a sandbox that is blocked from all network access — they cannot send your data anywhere.

Data sharing

None. We do not share your data with the developer or any third party. There is no server operated by AppLadle or Synapsin Gravy Workshop that receives your app data.

Your controls

  • Export: you can export your apps (.fsapp) and back up your data as an encrypted file (.fsbak) that only your passphrase can open.
  • Delete: you can delete individual profiles and their data in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

  • Internet — used only for the optional Create with Gemini request described above.
  • Notifications — used only to deliver reminders you schedule; requested when you first use a reminder.

Children

AppLadle is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone. Because the app collects no data at all, no personal information about children is gathered.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change. If a future version of AppLadle ever collects anything, we will update this page before it does and say so plainly.
